# MCP and A2A Hackathon - AWS Edition

Build multimodal AI agents capable of advanced reasoning and autonomous decision-making to effectively address and solve a complex, real-world enterprise challenge.

The agent(s) should use tools, take actions, and be demonstrable through an interactive UX, showcasing its ability to sense, think, plan, retrieve, and execute effectively.

## 🏛️ Judging & Submissions

### **Judging Criteria**

Projects will be judged on the following criteria (weighted equally)

- **Idea -** Unique value proposition delivered by a vertical-specific agent using advanced reasoning, action, and tool use.
- **Technical implementation** - Surprise and inspires judges and participants through the novel use of tools in a unique way.
- **Tool Use** - Integration of at least 3 sponsor tools to enable sophisticated agentic behavior
- **Presentation** - Present a live demo in 3-minutes that clearly demonstrates the agent’s impact. Keep it to 1 overview slide.
